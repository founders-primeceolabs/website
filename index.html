03PRIME OS

One method.
Three engines.

PRIME OS is CEO LAB’s diagnostic and operating method. It looks at Revenue, Operations, and Leadership without confusing the business lifecycle, the founder’s role, and the current constraint.

01

Revenue

How the business creates demand, converts opportunity, and produces profitable growth.

02

Operations

How the company delivers value consistently, profitably, and without constant intervention.

03

Leadership

How priorities are set, decisions are made, and capability expands beyond the founder.

A lower-scoring engine is a hypothesis to investigate with evidence—not a label or a verdict.

The leadership progression

The business changes when your role does.

PRIME OS helps founders see the next leadership and operating move—not force a generic stage label.

01

Doing the work

MVE

Your expertise carries the business. The next move is to make key work and knowledge less dependent on you.

02

Designing the system

Operator

You turn expertise into repeatable work, clear ownership, and a company that relies less on your personal intervention.

03

Leading the system

CEO

You build the leaders, priorities, and operating cadence that produce results beyond your daily intervention.

04

Allocating attention and capital

Owner

The company is increasingly able to perform without your daily presence, supporting enduring enterprise value.
